“Cooked” is a mathematical conclusion, not a post-exam feeling.
Reading the meter: Chilling means your 85% target has comfortable room. Needs work means it is alive, but future scores matter. Danger means the target is possible only with very strong remaining results. Mathematically cooked appears only when even perfect future marks cannot reach the target.

For unknown future marks, the site assumes the maximum is still available when calculating your ceiling. Bangla and English test fields use /100; every other subject uses /75. It never treats an unreported mark as zero. For ICT, there is no 1st-paper half: its one-paper result is the final subject result.
